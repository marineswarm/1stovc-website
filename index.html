Who we are

We are a Civil War living history unit portraying Company A of the 1st Ohio Volunteer Cavalry.

We ride, drill and camp the way they did — and we do the research to back it up. Our portrayal is built on the company's own records: muster cards, letters home, pension files and the company history. Wherever the records disagree, we say so.

Company A is our home company. The research is now moving on to Companies C, E and K.

Company A at a glance

  1. Aug 1861
    Mustered in at Camp Chase, Ohio. Three-year volunteers under Capt. John H. Robinson.
  2. Feb 1862
    Bloomery Furnace, Virginia. When the lead regiment faltered, Gen. Lander called out for the Ohio boys — and they went forward.
  3. 1862–1863
    Shenandoah Valley, the Peninsula, Second Bull Run, Gettysburg. Eleven official engagements on their record.
  4. 1864–1865
    Transferred west. Nashville, Gravelly Springs, the raid on Richmond, the pursuit of Jefferson Davis.
  5. Sep 1865
    Honorably discharged at Hilton Head, South Carolina.
