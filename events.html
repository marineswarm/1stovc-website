What to expect at an event

Living history events are three-day weekends in the field — Friday setup, Saturday and Sunday public programming, then pack out. You'll sleep in period-correct camp, cook on the fire, and drill or ride with the unit during the day. The public programs vary: some events include battle reenactments, others focus on camp life and demonstrations.

If you're thinking about joining and want to see what it's like before you buy any kit, the training camp in March is the right starting point. Come out, talk to the troopers, ask questions.
