Come out first

The best first step is to come out to an event and see camp before you commit to anything. Watch how we operate, talk to the troopers, ask questions. The training camp in March is set up exactly for this.

You don't need a horse, a uniform or any kit to show up and look around. You just need to show up.

What we do

We are a living history unit, not a club. The difference is in the standard. We portray real men from a real company based on real research. The kit has to be right, the drill has to be right, and when we don't know something for certain, we say so instead of guessing.

What that looks like in practice:

  • Three-day events, usually four to five a year
  • Mounted drill and camp life at events
  • Research work between events — pension files, muster cards, letters home
  • Public education at events: talking to visitors, explaining the history
  • Helping find and document the men who served in the company
